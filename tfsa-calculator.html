Why It Matters

The advantages of a TFSA

Simple in theory, easy to under-use in practice. Here's why it's worth actually maxing out.

Growth is 100% tax-free

Every dollar of interest, dividends, or capital gains inside a TFSA grows without being taxed — not this year, not when you withdraw it.

Withdrawals are tax-free too

Unlike an RRSP, money you pull out of a TFSA in retirement isn't added to your taxable income for the year.

Doesn't affect OAS or GIS

TFSA withdrawals aren't counted as income, so they won't trigger Old Age Security clawback or reduce income-tested benefits.

Room carries forward, forever

Unused contribution room never expires — it just keeps accumulating year after year, waiting for you to use it.

Fully flexible access

There's no penalty for withdrawing early, and whatever you take out gets added back to your contribution room the following January.

Useful at every life stage

An emergency fund, a house down payment, or a retirement account — a TFSA can be all three, and it changes roles as your life does.
