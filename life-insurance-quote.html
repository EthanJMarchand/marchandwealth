How It Works

What this quote actually tells you

The tool above pulls live, published rates from major Canadian life insurance carriers based on the details you enter — your province, birthdate, gender, smoker status, an estimated health class, the type of policy, and how much coverage you want. It's the same starting point I use with clients to see roughly where pricing lands before we go any further.

A few things affect the number more than people expect. Term length matters — a 10-year term costs less than a 20 or 30-year term for the same coverage, but locks in for a shorter window. Health class matters even more: the difference between Preferred Plus and Regular can be significant for the exact same amount of coverage, and your actual class isn't confirmed until underwriting looks at your full health picture, not just what you select in the tool. And permanent policies — whole life or Term to 100 — will always quote higher than term for the same face amount, because they're built to last your entire life instead of a fixed period.

Because pricing for identical coverage can vary meaningfully between insurers, comparing several side by side — rather than calling one company directly — is usually the difference between an average deal and a good one. If a number here doesn't look right for your situation, or you're not sure which option actually fits, that's exactly the kind of thing worth a quick conversation before you apply anywhere.
